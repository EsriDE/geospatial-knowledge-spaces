# Ukraine Battle Simulation

## Objective

Demonstrate how Tactical Core events can be transformed into operational knowledge.

---

## Scenario

Actors:

- Ukrainian Forces (UA)
- Russian Forces (RU)

Environment:

- Urban Area
- Semi-Urban Area
- Critical Infrastructure

---

## Simulation Inputs

- Unit locations
- Sensor observations
- Mission assignments
- Engagement events

---

## Simulation Outputs

- Engagement clusters
- Threat zones
- Frontline estimation
- Mission success indicators

---

## Visualization

- Time Slider
- Heatmaps
- Combat Events
- Knowledge Graphs

---

## Human Decision Points

The objective is not automated decision making.

The objective is explainable operational awareness.

---

## Technology Mapping

- Tactical Core feeds: unit locations, mission assignments, engagement events
- GeoAI: derives movement patterns, threat indicators, frontline estimation
- Geospatial Knowledge Space: fuses events into engagement clusters and threat zones
- Operational Knowledge Twin: maintains the confidence-scored operational picture
- Digital Battlespace: hosts the time slider, heatmaps, and knowledge graph views
