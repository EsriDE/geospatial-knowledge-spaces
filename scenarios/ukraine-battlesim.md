# Ukraine Battle Simulation

## Objective

Demonstrate how Tactical Core events can be transformed into operational knowledge.

---

## Scenario

Actors:

- Ukrainian Forces (UA)
- Russian Forces (RU)

Environment:

- Urban Area
- Semi-Urban Area
- Critical Infrastructure

---

## Simulation Inputs

- Unit locations
- Sensor observations
- Mission assignments
- Engagement events

---

## Simulation Outputs

- Engagement clusters
- Threat zones
- Frontline estimation
- Mission success indicators

---

## Visualization

- Time Slider
- Heatmaps
- Combat Events
- Knowledge Graphs

---

## Human Decision Points

The objective is not automated decision making.

The objective is explainable operational awareness.
