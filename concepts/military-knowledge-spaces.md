# Military Knowledge Spaces

## Definition

Military Knowledge Spaces transform isolated information systems into knowledge-driven environments.

Rather than asking:

"What data do we have?"

Military Knowledge Spaces ask:

"What do we know?"

---

## Core Idea

Information becomes knowledge through:

- Spatial context
- Temporal context
- Trust
- Relationships
- Operational relevance

---

## Core Capabilities

### Knowledge Integration

Link observations, reports, intelligence and sensor streams.

### Trust & Confidence

Assess confidence levels rather than presenting information as absolute truth.

### Scenario Development

Model alternative futures and possible courses of action.

### Dynamic Situational Awareness

Continuously update understanding of the operational environment.

### Edge Operations

Support operations in disconnected and degraded environments.

---

## Role of AI

### Agentic AI

Maintains and updates knowledge.

### GeoAI

Provides spatial reasoning.

The human remains the final decision authority.
